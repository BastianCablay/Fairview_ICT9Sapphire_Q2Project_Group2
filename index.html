Welcome to O.B. Montessori Center!


Education is a journey that helps children discover who they are, develop their abilities, and build a strong foundation for the future.

O.B. Montessori Center is a progressive pre-K to Grade 12 co-educational school that follows the educational philosophy of Dr. Maria Montessori. Through a child-centered approach, students are given the opportunity to learn through experience, explore their interests, and develop at their own pace.

We strive to nurture individuals who are independent, confident, and responsible, equipped with the knowledge and values they need to face life's challenges.

Explore our programs and learn more about a school community dedicated to helping every child grow, learn, and reach their full potential.
